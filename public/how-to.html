📋 Entering Scores
1
Tap Schedule and find your week Tap the row to see your matchup, then tap Score →
2
Select your team from the dropdown at the top You must have your team selected to confirm scores
3
Enter gross scores hole by hole for all four players
4
When the round is complete, tap Confirm Both teams must confirm to lock the result
⚠️ Don't confirm until all 9 holes are entered. Confirmed scores are locked and require admin to unlock.
